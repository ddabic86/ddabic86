### Operational architecture

| Domain | Capabilities |
|---|---|
| **Structures** | Organizations, regions, sites, buildings, floors, areas, rooms and assets. |
| **Identity** | People, professional profiles, memberships, roles and qualifications. |
| **Governance** | Permissions, access boundaries, responsibilities, approvals and audit. |
| **Operations** | Procedures, tasks, steps, dependencies, assignments and execution. |
| **Intelligence** | Dynamic risk, priorities, irregularities, escalation and follow-up. |
| **Workforce** | Shifts, availability, presence, competencies and scheduling. |
| **Resources** | Equipment, inventory, vehicles and material movements. |
| **Geography** | Locations, live positioning, routing and travel. |
| **Communication** | Real-time events, messaging and notifications. |
| **Commerce** | Services, orders, subscriptions, billing and payments. |

Every domain is interconnected. Changes in operational state, staffing, risk and availability are reflected in real time across the relevant workflows and users.

### AVGVSTVS — Operational engine

Governs the execution of operations through configurable procedures, dependencies, qualifications, evidence and dynamic risk.

- Live operational command and execution
- Conditional procedures and automatically generated follow-up operations
- Workforce planning and qualification-based assignments
- Dynamic risk, escalation and prioritization
- Operational continuity across people, locations and shifts

## System architecture

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    subgraph PXNT
        direction TB
        MDXT["MDXT · Healthcare"] --> A["AVGVSTVS · Operational execution"]
        BBGI["BBGI · Sites"] --> A
        ECPK["ECPK · Services"] --> M["MERCVRIVS · Service orchestration"]
        A --> K["Shared Kernel"]
        M --> K
        K --- S["Identity · Structures · Governance · Events · Resources · Commerce"]
    end
```

The architecture is built around relational domain modeling, independent execution engines, reusable infrastructure and isolated organizational data.

Engineering foundations include deterministic business logic, transactional auditing, scoped authorization, concurrency control, idempotency and explainable risk calculations.

AI assists with document processing, information extraction, translation and content generation without controlling operational decisions.
